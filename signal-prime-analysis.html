UX & Conversion Analysis Report

Website Review for signal-prime.com

Date: February 14, 2026 Analysis Type: Full Funnel Review
Overall Score: 7/10
Executive Summary: Primelis Signal is a lead-generation tool targeting brands spending $15K+/month on branded search. The tool analyzes potential savings from optimizing branded ad spend. The core funnel is well-designed with a clever "free analysis" hook, but lacks trust signals and has conversion optimization gaps on the results page.

Funnel Overview

The site uses a 4-step funnel: Input → Analysis → Results → Next Steps

  • Step 1 (Input): User enters domain + selects monthly spend range
  • Step 2 (Analysis): Live SERP analysis with progress indicators (~30-40 seconds)
  • Step 3 (Results): Shows key metrics (top of page %, solo-bidder %, savings %)
  • Step 4 (Next Steps): CTAs to book meeting or receive results via email

What Works Well

1. Brilliant Value Proposition

  • "See how much your branded search is really costing you — in under a minute"
  • Immediate, clear benefit with low time commitment
  • Targets a real pain point (wasted branded spend)

2. Frictionless Entry

  • Only 2 inputs required: domain + spend range
  • No email/signup gate before seeing results
  • Smart domain validation with "Looks good" + brand detection feedback

3. Excellent Loading Experience

  • Progress stepper (1→2→3→4) sets expectations
  • Live "Signal Feed" with real-time updates feels authentic
  • Animated progress bar with task breakdown (Mapping → Detecting → Estimating)

4. Strong Visual Design

  • Dark mode with purple accent creates premium tech feel
  • Clean typography and generous whitespace
  • Brand logo auto-fetched adds personalization

5. Results Presentation

  • Key metrics front and center (84% top of page, 43% savings)
  • Simple before/after chart is instantly understandable
  • "Directional preview, not a billing-impact estimate" = good expectation management

6. Clear CTAs at Conversion Point

  • "Book a meeting" (primary) vs "Send me the results by email" (secondary)
  • Low-commitment email option captures hesitant leads

Areas for Improvement

1. Above-the-Fold Issues

Problem Impact Recommended Fix
CTA button starts disabled/gray Users may not realize they need to fill fields Add micro-copy like "Enter your domain to begin"
No visible social proof Reduces trust for first-time visitors Add "Trusted by 500+ brands" or client logos

2. Mobile Experience

  • Spend range buttons stack awkwardly on mobile
  • Consider a dropdown or slider for mobile devices
  • "Enter exact spend" toggle is too small to tap easily

3. Missing Trust Signals

  • No client logos on landing page
  • No testimonials or case studies
  • No "As seen in" press mentions
  • The "Learn more" link goes to primelis.com but there's no explanation of who Primelis is

4. Funnel Friction Points

Step Issue
Domain input No autocomplete or suggestions
Spend selection Highlighting of selected option could be clearer (border is subtle)
Loading state 30-40 seconds is long — consider background processing + email delivery

5. Results Page Gaps

  • "Savings: 43%" lacks context — 43% of what dollar amount?
  • No breakdown of how they'll achieve the savings
  • "Back to analysis" link feels like an escape hatch, not a next step
  • Missing urgency/scarcity elements ("Limited spots for Q1 onboarding")

6. Technical/SEO Issues

  • Single Page App with no SSR = poor SEO (ironic for a search company)
  • No <noscript> fallback for users without JavaScript
  • Page title doesn't change between funnel steps

7. Conversion Optimization Gaps

  • No exit-intent popup to capture abandoning visitors
  • No chatbot or live support for questions
  • Email capture at the end should be a form field, not just a link

Recommended Quick Wins

  1. Add social proof to homepage (client logos, "500+ brands analyzed")
  2. Improve button state feedback — make it obvious when the form is complete
  3. Personalize results with actual dollar amounts based on their spend range
  4. Add urgency to booking CTA ("Limited availability this month")
  5. Implement SSR for the landing page (even just the hero section)
  6. A/B test gating results behind email vs. current ungated approach

Overall Verdict

This is a well-designed lead-gen tool with a clever hook. The interactive analysis creates perceived value before asking for contact info. The main weaknesses are lack of trust signals and some missed conversion optimization opportunities on the results page.

The core concept — "free analysis tool → impressive results → book a call" — is solid and well-executed. With the recommended improvements, conversion rates could increase significantly.
